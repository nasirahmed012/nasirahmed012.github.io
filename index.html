01 About

I am currently serving as a Lecturer of Chemistry at St. Gregory's High School & College, a role I began in January 2026. My academic background is rooted at the University of Barishal, where I completed both my BS (Honors) and MS in Chemistry, graduating at the top of my department each time.

I'm aspiring to be part of an institution that fosters a supportive and engaging learning environment — where teaching goes beyond lectures to inspire curiosity and critical thinking. I aim to join spaces that offer student-centered learning, along with the resources and autonomy to teach effectively.

Outside the classroom, I've spent years active in Bangladesh's chemistry research and student community — from polymer synthesis research to leadership roles in ChemFusion, a national chemistry-focused organization.
